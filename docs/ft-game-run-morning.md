# FT Stock Picking Game: morning check (H2) and midday check (H6)

Parts H2 and H6 of the playbook (version 6.4, 17 Oct 2026: the check moves to 06:36 UK and the cut-off is treated as 07:00 UK until the FT confirms it; version 6, 15 Oct 2026). Read with the core playbook (`claude/ft-game-playbook.md`), the rules (`claude/ft-game-rules.md`), the data doc (`claude/ft-game-data.md`) and the state doc.

## H2. Morning check (06:36 UK, Monday to Friday)

Speed matters: Tim has to enter any change before 07:00 UK (part C). Finish within about ten minutes, so that a revision reaches him by about 06:50 UK, with 10 to 15 searches and fetches sent together, and lead with the verdict. Tim hears from this run only when it revises last night's order (part I, "When Tim is told").

1. Read the state doc. If it shows no portfolio and no pending order, give a short pre-open market read only. In the same turn fetch `stocks.json`, `ledger.json` and the script (G1; the panel file is not needed) and the two tradingeconomics pages: the ledger's open `daycall` rows hold last night's call. On a Monday, also read the "Weekend log" in section 7 of the state doc and check how oil and US index futures have traded since they reopened at 23:00 UK on Sunday: that first reaction says more than any headline.
2. Check, from current-session sources only (UK announcements at 07:00 are usually not out yet at 06:36; they are in the opening price anyway, and the next evening review weighs them; for any that are out, search the company's name with "RNS" or "trading update"): US after-hours results, guidance and prices for the holdings and for any watchlist stock that reported or had news overnight (quote pages for those only, to keep within the pacing rule); the overnight session (Asia, index futures, the US 10-year yield, oil with any Iran or Hormuz news, the dollar); UK regulatory announcements at 07:00; overnight rating and target changes; today's schedule in UK time. US pre-market and after-hours quotes with timestamps are on the stockanalysis.com quote pages.
3. Confirm last night's order unless something material and new changes the case. Do not tinker.
   - A qualifying news momentum event that landed after the evening review, on a stock that already showed follow-through in its last full session, may be added at 5% to 10% in place of the weakest holding or cash. Brand-new news with no session behind it is flagged for tonight, not chased. The same holds for results: an after-hours beat-and-raise has no reaction session yet, so it waits for tonight's drift scan; the pre-market price already holds the gap the order would pay.
   - If a crypto-linked stock is held or is in last night's order (`crypto_link` is "yes" in stocks.json), fetch the live Bitcoin price (part F) with the other checks. The stored close is the `macro` row with `ticker` "btc" in the ledger. A fall of 8% or more since that close on a session when the position is due to be bought is a reason to leave it out of this morning's order; for a position already held the gap cannot be dodged, so it changes nothing by itself.
   - Remove a momentum or drift position (its `note` starts "Momentum position:" or "Drift position:") if last night's order did not already do so and its row in stocks.json shows a "heavy-volume down day" flag, or a `from_open_pct` below -2 with a negative `day_pct`. The fuller exit test belongs to the evening review.
4. If the order is revised, send the verdict and the full order through SendUserMessage at once, with the reason in one sentence and one line on the day call: last night's figure, the overnight moves in the 10-year yield and Brent, and whether the call stands. If the order is not revised, send nothing through SendUserMessage. That holds whether last night was a change evening or a quiet one: last night's message already told Tim what to enter.
5. Only if the order changed: write order.json (`effective`, `entered`, `order_for`, `order_reason`, and the full `order` with `name`, `weight`, `confidence`), run `reorder`, publish the two files (G5), and update the state doc (pending order, trade log row, review log line). Every stock in a morning order must already be in stocks.json.
6. After the order has gone out, record the overnight macro picture: write quotes.json with `date` (today), empty `quotes`, `macro_now` (`as_of` such as "07:35 UK" and `values` for `us10y`, `uk10y`, `de10y`, `jp10y`, `brent`, `wti`, `gold`, and `btc` and `eth` from the live Coinbase price) and, only if the overnight news or moves justify it, `daycall` (`date` today, `adjust` and `port_adjust` of up to 5 points either way, and a one-sentence `reason`). If step 5 ran, also give `alerts` one row with `stock` "Order", today's date, the UK time now as `time_uk`, and text "Revised at <time> for today's open: <the change in a few words>". Run `refresh` with `--ledger` (on the files step 5 produced, if it ran) and publish `ledger.json`, using the version number that step 5's update returned if it ran. This step never holds up step 4.
7. Final message: the morning output below, in its revised or its unchanged form.

### Output

When the order stands: nothing through SendUserMessage, and the final message is exactly "No change this morning. Nothing to enter.", followed by one more line only if something matters for tonight's review.

When it revises the order: (1) verdict in the first line, "Revised order: enter this instead", with the reason in one sentence; (2) the full order table to enter before 07:00 UK: Stock | Weight | Confidence (not calibrated) | Change versus current, including cash; (3) three to six bullets on what happened overnight and whether it matters, including the moves in the 10-year yield and Brent, whether last night's green-or-red call stands, analyst changes and events flagged for tonight; (4) today's key times in UK time; (5) sources. Short enough to read in two minutes.

## H6. Midday check (12:40 UK, Monday to Friday; switched off)

Not in use. The game's rules say an order entered during the trading day takes effect on the next trading day (part C), so a midday change cannot fill the same day. It acts only if section 8 of the state doc says Tim has seen the game fill a US order entered after 08:00 UK at the same day's US open. If it does not say so, reply "No change. Midday check is off until the US cut-off is confirmed." and stop.

1. Scan the morning's US broker rating changes and pre-market movers for holdings and watchlist stocks, and any results released before the US open.
2. A change is allowed only for US-listed stocks and only under the news momentum rule or the exit rule in part E, or to exit before a gap that the pre-market price already shows will breach the exit rule. Do not tinker.
3. If nothing qualifies, reply "No change. No midday change." and send nothing. If something does, send the revised full order through SendUserMessage with the time by which it must be entered (before the US open), then record it with `reorder` and publish as in H2 step 5.
