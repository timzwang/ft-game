        Definitions
        Points
        Weight × move. A 15% position that rises 2% adds 0.30 points to the portfolio.
        Score and strength
        Out of 100: trend 40, recent behaviour 20, catalyst 20, analyst changes 20. Strong is 60 or more above the 50-day average with no downtrend; weak is under 45 or a downtrend. Under the endgame stance the score no longer decides what is bought.
        Conviction
        The confidence anchor from the latest nightly rating. Not calibrated: a ranking, not a chance of beating the S&P 500.
        Gap to top 2%
        The return of the player nearest the top 2% line, as last read off the FT site, minus this portfolio's estimated return now.
        Order
        Enter a changed order on the FT site before 08:00 UK, the FT's cut-off, UK local time all year. Changes fill at the next open.
        Sources
        Prices, US yields and coins: the price feed (Yahoo Finance daily bars, the US Treasury, Coinbase). Other yields: CNBC and the Japanese Ministry of Finance. Ranks and FT values: read off the FT site by Tim.
