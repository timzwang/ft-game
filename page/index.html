        Definitions and sources
        Points
        Weight × move. A 15% position that rises 2% adds 0.30 points to the portfolio.
        Score and band
        Out of 100 from the pipeline: trend 40, recent behaviour 20, catalyst 20, analyst changes 20. Strong is 60 or more in an uptrend; weak is under 45 or a downtrend.
        Conviction
        The confidence anchor from the latest nightly rating: the estimated chance of beating the S&P 500 over five sessions. Not yet calibrated: treat it as a ranking, not a probability.
        vs S&P 500, 20d
        The stock's return over its last 20 stored sessions minus the S&P 500's (SPY) over the same dates, in percentage points. Sector benchmarks are not stored, so this is market-relative, not sector-relative.
        Order states
        Proposed: issued by a review, to enter before 08:00 UK. Pending fill: assumed entered, waiting for the open. Filled: past the open, estimate only. Confirmed: an FT site value has been read since the fill.

        US prices: stockanalysis.com. UK prices: lse.co.uk. US yields: US Treasury. Other yields and commodities: tradingeconomics.com. Coins: Coinbase. Ranks and FT values: read off the FT site by Tim.
